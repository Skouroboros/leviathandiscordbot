LeviathanBot – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LeviathanBot collects, uses and stores information when used on Discord servers.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on which features are enabled, LeviathanBot may process:

  • Discord user IDs, usernames and server IDs.
  • Discord channel and message information needed for bot functionality.
  • World of Warcraft character names and related game information supplied by users.
  • Raid signups, selected roles, specializations and event attendance.
  • Crafting requests and crafting-related information.
  • Dungeon group and party information.
  • Bot configuration and server preferences.
  • Moderation and activity logs where logging features are enabled.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to:

  • Provide the bot's functionality.
  • Manage raid signups and community events.
  • Handle crafting and dungeon group requests.
  • Manage server settings and permissions.
  • Maintain security and investigate misuse.
  • Improve reliability and troubleshoot errors.

4. Data Storage and Security

Depending on the deployment and enabled features, information may be stored in a local database, including SQLite, to maintain settings and functionality between restarts.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. However, no storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Sharing

LeviathanBot does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed through Discord and third-party services or APIs where necessary to provide specific features. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the bot's functionality, security or applicable legal obligations.

Some information may be removed automatically through configured retention or cleanup features. Users or server administrators may request deletion of information where appropriate and technically possible.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable data protection law, including the GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information.

You may also have the right to object to certain processing or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children's Privacy

LeviathanBot is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable local laws.

9. Third-Party Services

LeviathanBot relies on Discord and may interact with external services or APIs. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

LeviathanBot is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment or Discord Inc.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be available on this page.
